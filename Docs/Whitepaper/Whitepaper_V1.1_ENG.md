1. Project Overview

  What is EXO?

    EXO is a digital asset built upon a community-driven operating model.

    All project operations are guided by the following principles:

    • Transparency
    • Accountability
    • Long-Term Stability
    • Community Communication
    • Gradual Decentralization

    EXO respects the independent judgment of all participants and discourages excessive expectations and irresponsible speculation.

    The management team does not guarantee profits, and all participants are expected to make their own informed decisions.

8. Governance Policy

    This policy has been established to define the operating principles that support transparency, stability, 
    and gradual decentralization within the EXO ecosystem.
    EXO is a community-driven project and prioritizes transparency and accountability in the exercise of token supply authority 
    and smart contract administrative permissions.
    The governance framework is intended to encourage community participation while maintaining responsible project management.
    Major decisions affecting the ecosystem may be subject to public discussion, community feedback, 
    and voting procedures as defined in this whitepaper.
    The long-term goal of EXO governance is to support a sustainable transition toward greater community participation 
    and decentralization while preserving ecosystem stability.

9. Voting Rights

    EXOciety promotes a governance model based on community participation.
    
    Voting may be conducted on major project matters, including ecosystem development, partnerships, community operations, 
    and other significant issues affecting the project.
    
    Voting rights are granted on the principle of one vote per eligible account within each official community, regardless of token holdings, wallet count, or asset size.
    The official communities eligible to participate in voting, as well as voting requirements, will be announced 
    by the management team prior to each vote.
    
    A single proposal may be voted on across multiple official communities. The final outcome will be determined 
    by aggregating all valid votes collected from the participating communities.
   
    EXOciety seeks to prevent excessive influence by large token holders and supports a governance model 
    that values community participation and member feedback.

    Voting eligibility, vote-counting methods, and governance procedures may be modified or 
    supplemented as the ecosystem evolves and operational needs change.

10. Community Voting Policy

  Voting Topics

    • Additional Minting
    • Token Burning
    • Additional Liquidity Provision
    • Use of Defense Allocation
    • Major Tokenomics Changes
    • Major Operational Policy Changes
    • Other matters deemed significant by the management team

  Voting Announcement

    The following information will be disclosed prior to voting:

    • Proposal Details
    • Purpose
    • Expected Benefits
    • Potential Impact
    • Voting Start Date
    • Voting End Date
    • Voting Period

    The voting period will be determined by the management team based on the importance and urgency of the proposal.

  Approval Criteria

    • Approval: 50.1% or more of valid votes in favor
    • Rejection: Less than 50.1% of valid votes in favor

    The approval percentage shall be calculated based on the total number of valid votes cast in favor 
    of or against the proposal. Abstentions shall be excluded from the approval percentage calculation.

  Result Disclosure

    The following voting results will be publicly disclosed:

    • Total Participants
    • Votes in Favor
    • Votes Against
    • Abstentions (if applicable)
    • Final Approval Percentage
    • Approved or Rejected Status

  Core Principle

    The management team respects the outcome of community voting 
    and seeks to reflect the collective will of the community in project operations.

11. Privacy and Anonymity Protection

    EXOciety's management team regards anonymity protection 
    and privacy protection as core principles of ecosystem operations.
    
    Participants may engage in ecosystem activities, community participation, 
    and governance processes without publicly disclosing their real identity.

    Except where required by lawful investigations, court orders, or other legally authorized enforcement procedures, 
    the management team will not disclose, sell, share, or provide participant information under its control to third parties.

    If participants voluntarily provide information to affiliated services or partner organizations, such disclosures 
    are considered a matter between the participant and the respective organization 
    and shall not be regarded as information disclosure by EXOciety.

    EXOciety values participant contributions and opinions above personal identity 
    and strives to operate in a manner that minimizes unnecessary intrusion into personal privacy.

12. Minting Policy

    The EXO smart contract currently contains a minting function.
    However, the fundamental principle of the project is not to conduct additional minting.
  
    The minting authority is retained solely to address situations such as:

    • Token Migration
    • Blockchain Network Migration
    • Token Swap Operations
    • Ecosystem Structural Changes
    • Exceptional circumstances recognized and approved by the community

    No additional minting will be conducted without community approval.

13. Burning Policy

    The EXO smart contract currently contains a burning function.
    However, the fundamental principle of the project is not to conduct arbitrary token burning.
    If a burn is proposed, the following information will be disclosed and the following procedures will be carried out:

    • Purpose of the Burn
    • Burn Amount
    • Expected Effects
    • Schedule
    • Community Discussion
    • Community Voting

    These procedures must be completed before any burn is carried out.
    All burn records will be publicly disclosed and verifiable on-chain.

14. Ownership Policy

    EXO currently maintains smart contract ownership authority.

    Ownership serves as the minimum level of administrative authority required 
    for project operations, technical management, and ecosystem stability.

    Ownership is not intended for manipulating token supply or influencing market conditions. 
    Its purpose is limited to project protection, maintenance, and operational management.

    Whenever reasonably possible, the management team will disclose the use 
    of ownership-related permissions to the community.

15. Renounce Ownership Policy

    EXO does not currently plan to renounce contract ownership.
    The management team does not intend to retain ownership authority permanently.
    Once the project has reached an appropriate level of stability and sufficient discussion has taken place within the community, 
    the possibility of renouncing ownership may be considered.
