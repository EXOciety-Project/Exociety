# Official Declaration

This whitepaper has been prepared to explain the operating principles, tokenomics, governance policies, 

and community operating standards of the EXO project.

EXO is not a project created to promise short-term price appreciation or financial returns.

EXO aims to operate as a sustainable project based on transparent information disclosure, responsible management, 

and communication with the community.

EXO values the independence of its community and fairness among its members. 

It will not yield to pressure from large external entities or specific interested parties, 

and it maintains the fundamental principle of avoiding unreasonable compromises that undermine the community's principles 

and fairness.

This whitepaper distinguishes between matters that have currently been confirmed 

and operating principles that are currently being pursued.

Unconfirmed future plans, roadmaps whose implementation remains uncertain, projected returns, 

and content intended to encourage investment will not be presented as established facts.

# 4. Liquidity Reserve

## Allocated Amount

A total of 60 Billion EXO.

## Operating Principles

The Liquidity Reserve is managed for the purpose of securing liquidity for the EXO ecosystem and establishing a long-term market foundation.

The initial liquidity pool is configured on a USDC basis.

This choice reflects the importance placed on providing a stable participation environment for the community and ensuring greater predictability in liquidity management.

USDC was selected as the initial liquidity asset to reduce reliance on highly volatile assets and to use a relatively stable value reference during liquidity preparation and management.

However, USDC also carries risks related to its issuer, market conditions, and potential depegging. It should not be considered a risk-free asset.

The Liquidity Reserve will not be supplied in its entirety at once. As a fundamental principle, it will be utilized gradually, taking project growth and market conditions into consideration.

If additional liquidity provision is deemed necessary, the following factors will be considered:

- Purpose of the provision
- Amount to be supplied
- Timing of the provision
- Market conditions
- Expected effects

Prior disclosure and the collection of community feedback are fundamental principles for major liquidity policies.

## Use and Sale of Tokens to Secure Liquidity

When necessary, tokens from the Liquidity Reserve may be sold in the market to obtain assets required for liquidity provision, such as USDC.

Such sales will be limited to purposes related to securing liquidity for the EXO ecosystem and establishing a long-term operational foundation.

Before any sale, the project team will disclose the following information to the community:

- Purpose of the sale
- Intended sale amount
- Expected sale period
- Method of sale
- Estimated amount of assets to be obtained
- Plan for supplying the acquired assets to liquidity pools

The final decision on whether to proceed with the sale will be made after collecting community feedback and conducting a community vote.

After the sale has been completed, the following information will be disclosed:

- Actual amount sold
- Sale execution records
- Types and amounts of assets acquired
- Liquidity pool supply records

Assets obtained through the sale will be managed in accordance with the publicly disclosed purpose and will not be diverted to purposes unrelated to the approved objectives.

Sales of tokens from the Liquidity Reserve are not intended for personal profit-taking or short-term market price adjustments.

Such sales will be conducted solely for the purpose of securing liquidity for the EXO ecosystem and establishing a long-term operational foundation.

## Core Principles

Liquidity provision and the sale of tokens to secure liquidity will be carried out gradually, based on communication and voting with the community.

Liquidity provision will not be used as a means of artificially manipulating market prices.

# 6. Defense Reserve

## Allocated Amount

A total of 20 Billion EXO.

- 5 defense wallets will be operated.
- 4 Billion EXO will be held in each wallet.

## Purpose

The Defense Reserve is an emergency asset intended to protect the EXO ecosystem and maintain its stability.

If additional minting takes place, the policy of allocating 20% of the minted amount to the Defense Reserve will also apply.

## Usage Principles

The Defense Reserve may only be used for defensive purposes intended to protect the EXO ecosystem and its community.

As a fundamental principle, the use of the Defense Reserve is prohibited in all circumstances other than those involving defensive purposes.

General development and operations, marketing, rewards, or other business needs do not constitute valid reasons for using the Defense Reserve.

## Usage Procedure

If the use of the Defense Reserve becomes necessary, the following information will, as a fundamental principle, be disclosed:

- Purpose of use
- Amount to be used
- Date and time of use
- Expected effects

Final execution will then take place after collecting community feedback and conducting a community vote.

## Emergency Defensive Response

Emergency use may be considered only when an actual threat to the EXO ecosystem or its community arises and immediate defensive action is necessary, including situations such as:

- Serious circumstances threatening the continued existence of the project or community
- Malicious attacks or security incidents
- Significant liquidity crises requiring defensive measures
- Other situations requiring urgent defensive action to protect the ecosystem

Emergency responses are also limited to defensive purposes and must not be interpreted as an exception permitting use unrelated to defense.

After an emergency response, the following information will be disclosed:

- Purpose of use
- Amount used
- Date and time of use
- Results of the action

## Core Declaration

The Defense Reserve exists to defend the project.

As a fundamental principle, use unrelated to defensive purposes is not permitted.

# 8. Governance Policy

This policy has been established to define the operating principles for transparency, stability, and long-term decentralization within the EXO ecosystem.

EXO aims to be a community-centered project and places the highest priority on transparency and accountability in matters concerning token supply, smart contract permissions, and ecosystem financial management.

Governance is conducted based on community participation and feedback, while also taking the project's long-term stability and sustainability into consideration.

# 9. Voting Rights

Exociety aims to establish a governance system based on community participation.

Votes may be conducted on major project policies, ecosystem development directions, community operations, and other important matters.

As a fundamental principle, each account that meets the official community voting eligibility requirements is entitled to one vote, regardless of the amount of tokens held, the number of wallets, or the size of its assets.

The official communities in which voting will take place and the eligibility requirements for participation will be announced in advance by the project team.

Voting on the same matter may take place across multiple official communities. The final result will be determined by combining the valid votes counted across those communities.

Exociety discourages the concentration of influence among a small number of large holders and seeks a governance structure that respects community participation and the opinions of its members.

Voting eligibility, counting methods, and detailed operating policies may be revised or supplemented as the ecosystem grows and operational needs evolve.

- Details of the proposal

- Expected effects
- Expected impact
- Voting start date
- Voting end date

## Voting Period

The voting period will be determined separately by the project team, taking into consideration the importance and urgency of each proposal.

## Approval Criteria

- 50.1% or more approval based on valid votes: Approved
- Less than 50.1% approval based on valid votes: Rejected

The approval percentage will be calculated based on the total number of valid votes cast either in favor of or against the proposal.

Abstentions will be excluded from the approval percentage calculation.

## Disclosure of Results

- Relevant community
- Total number of participants
- Number of votes in favor
- Number of votes against
- Number of abstentions, if applicable
- Final approval percentage
- Whether the proposal was approved or rejected

The project team respects voting results and aims to reflect the community's decisions in project operations.

# 11. Privacy and Anonymity Protection

The Exociety project team considers the protection of anonymity and privacy to be fundamental principles of ecosystem operations.

Participants may engage in ecosystem activities, community participation, and governance processes without disclosing their real names.

Except where disclosure is required by lawful investigations, court orders, or other legitimate legal enforcement procedures in accordance with applicable laws, the project team will not disclose, sell, share, or provide participant information to third parties.

If participants voluntarily provide their information to a service provider or partner while using services associated with Exociety, this constitutes a relationship between the participant and that service provider or partner and will not be regarded as an act of information disclosure by Exociety.

Exociety values participants' opinions and contributions over their identities and will operate in a manner that avoids infringing upon personal privacy to the greatest extent possible.

The EXO contract is based on a direct deployment structure without Proxy or upgrade functionality.

Accordingly, post-deployment upgrades to the contract code itself are not supported.

As a fundamental principle, records of the use of operating permissions will be disclosed to the community to the extent possible.

# 15. Ownership Renunciation Policy

EXO will not renounce Ownership during its initial operating phase.

Ownership will be retained to maintain the minting and burning functions necessary for the project's initial growth and ecosystem development.

In the long term, the goal is to terminate additional minting upon reaching the maximum supply and to proceed with Ownership Renunciation once the project has reached a sufficient level of stability.

## Full Autonomy Following Ownership Renunciation

Ownership Renunciation is not merely a procedure for relinquishing the project team's permissions.

It represents the transition of the EXO ecosystem into a fully autonomous phase in which it operates independently, centered on the community and its participants, without relying on the administrative permissions of a specific operator or centralized management entity.

Once Ownership Renunciation has been completed, the following policies will apply:

- Permanent deactivation of the minting function
- Permanent deactivation of the burning function
- Permanent extinction of all administrative permissions dependent on Ownership
- No possibility of regranting or reacquiring administrative permissions
- No one, including the project team, will be able to exercise permissions that have been extinguished

After Ownership has been renounced, no one will be able to intervene in the contract using administrative permissions or restore previously extinguished permissions.

The EXO ecosystem aims to operate under a fully autonomous structure that does not depend on the authority of any specific operator.

As Ownership Renunciation is an irreversible decision, sufficient review and communication with the community are fundamental principles before proceeding.

# 16. Transaction Tax and Financial Management Policy

## Transaction Tax Operations

EXO operates a transaction tax policy as a means of securing financial resources for the continuous operation and development of the ecosystem.

The currently designed transaction tax rate is 0.2% for ordinary transactions.

EXO applies the same transaction tax rate to ensure fairness in taxation among founder, development and operations, event, defense reserve, and general participant wallets.

A transaction tax of 0.2% applies to all ordinary transactions and transfers, except for transactions in which the Liquidity Reserve wallet or the transaction tax receiving wallet is the sender.

As a fundamental principle, Exociety does not provide separate transaction tax privileges exclusively to specific individuals, the founder, or the project team.

Transaction tax revenue is primarily intended to be used as a funding source for project operations and development.

Transaction taxes shall be managed independently, without requiring separate approval or external intervention. 

However, relevant operational matters shall be publicly disclosed to ensure transparency.

Detailed transaction tax functions and additional application policies will be specified separately in future whitepaper updates.

The actual scope of application and detailed conditions will be based on the smart contract implementation and official public materials.

As a member of the Monad ecosystem, EXO seeks to contribute to the development of the network and the expansion of its ecosystem.

However, any purchase of MON will be assessed in consideration of market conditions and the project's financial soundness. As a fundamental principle, excessive or financially unreasonable purchases will not be made.

## Liquidity Reinvestment

If resources become available for allocation to liquidity pools through MON-related operations, the general direction is to use those resources to strengthen EXO's USDC-based liquidity pools.

Through this approach, EXO seeks to gradually expand its liquidity foundation and pursue long-term operational stability.

Specific asset management criteria and execution procedures will be determined in consideration of market conditions and the project's operational circumstances.

## Core Principles

EXO pursues both the sustainable growth of its own ecosystem and mutual growth with the Monad ecosystem.

All financial management decisions will give priority to the project's long-term stability and ecosystem development.
